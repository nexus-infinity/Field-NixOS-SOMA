## Key Corrections vs Registry

- **End Room** (`indoor.end_room`) = the Rumpus in drawings — timber extension on stumps at far end of bedroom wing.  
  JB confirmed: **this is where he sits**, 1 step down from Bedroom 4.  
  Section drawing (IMG_9257) confirms: stumps, pine lining, hardwood floor.  
  **NOT the same as the Backroom.**

- **Backroom** (`outbuild.backroom`) = separate outbuilding, NE corner of block, near/around the carport.  
  3 steps UP. Large games/utility room with its own terrace.  
  This building does NOT appear clearly in the main 1988 floor plans — likely a different build/addition.

- **Address**: 10 Watts Parade, Mount Eliza, VIC (confirmed from title block on drawings).

- **Ducted heating**: Installed new in this 1988 renovation (demolition plan shows slab cut for registers).  
  Intake + thermostat: Family Room. Serves: all bedrooms (which are BELOW Family Room level).  
  End Room: NOT served by ducted heating.

- **Terrace**: Labelled "existing" in 1988 proposed plan — was already built, eave-edge question was unresolved at drawing stage.
