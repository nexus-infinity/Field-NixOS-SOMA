# Potential only

None of these shapes does anything now. The services sleep. There is no clock, no neighbour check, no weight, and no directory on the machine. What follows is what each shape could do, if it were seated. Potential is not a present action.

A frequency is not an address. Dojo's tetrahedron is not a SOMA address. It appears inside `sacred_geometry/metatron_cube_translator.nix` and stays on Dojo's side of the page.

## What a flake would receive, and does not receive now

Once seated, a flake would receive its prime, its volume path, its DNA, the two neighbours that would have to agree, and its tick. It would not receive a Dojo model, a frequency as a name, or a hexagon as a count of chambers.

The centre, prime 23, would be the listener. It would not be a tenth copy of the other eight, and it would not be the hexagon.

None of that is happening.
