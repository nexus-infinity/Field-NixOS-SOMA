# Three paths

These are PEP addresses. They are not labels, and they are not Dojo.

Dojo keeps the shelf it already has. The seven public models on misterJB stay there. They are not copied into a chamber because a frequency matches. The chamber ownership matrix in Field-MacOS-DOJO is left as it is. It belongs to that sovereign.

| Address | What it holds | What it does not hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ghost/source/` | The original weight. It does not change. | A chamber. A Dojo model. A living copy. |
| `chambers/<prime>/volume/models/` | The living weight of that prime only. This is the file the chamber opens. | Another prime. A catalogue. A misterJB model. |
| `chambers/<prime>/volume/memory/` | What that chamber has done. | Another chamber's memory. |

A list of models may point at these paths. The list is not a path.

The primes are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23. A chamber is addressed by its prime. A frequency is not an identity.

Nothing has been moved. The directories are the address. The weights are not here yet.
