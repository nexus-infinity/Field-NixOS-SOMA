# A SOMA library, beside the Trident

Potential only. Nothing here is loaded.

The Trident libraries belong to Dojo. They answer four questions for that sovereign, and they stay there.

| Question | Trident | File it keeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | ● OB1 | `resonance_map.json` |
| Why it belongs | ▼ TATA | `sovereign_law.json` |
| How it relates | ▲ ATLAS | `symbolic_links.json`, `ontological_layers.json`, `temporal_cycles.json` |
| What it becomes | ◼ DOJO | `manifest_record.json` |

SOMA is not a fifth file in that set. The Trident note already places SOMA as what encases the four, not as one of them.

A SOMA library would be one shared declaration, called by nine flakes. Same questions. Different organs. Not those JSON files, and not a Dojo model.

| Question | What the library would answer for a flake | What it would not be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | The prime, and the volume path. | OB1's resonance map. |
| Why it belongs | The DNA. Genotype, identity, state, phenotype. | TATA's law file. |
| How it relates | The tick, and the two neighbours. | ATLAS's compass files. |
| What it opens | The living weight in that volume. The source stays in Ghost. | Dojo's manifest, and the misterJB shelf. |

Shared, the way a core library is shared: a reader for the DNA, a resolver for the two paths, the prime as a tick, and the neighbour check. Not shared: the weight, the memory, and anything Dojo stores.

Nine callers. One library. The centre, prime 23, would call it as the listener, not as a fourth vertex of the Trident.
